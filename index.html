Quillblade Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 (version 1.2, which adds optional rewarded ads)

This policy explains how the Android game Quillblade ("the app", "we", "us"), made by Kaselca Apps, handles information. In short, we do not collect your personal data ourselves. The only outside service that does is Google AdMob, which shows the ads described in section 4.

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • Reward videos play only when you choose to watch one, to unlock a Legend look or to get back up after a knockout. Now and then a short ad plays between pages (Quillblade Premium removes it). There are no banner ads.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • No analytics and no tracking by us. The only third-party SDKs are Google Play Billing and Google AdMob (with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent).
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your phone: unlocked pages, stars, scores, coins, upgrades, cosmetics you own, your settings (sound, music, graphics, vibration), and a short list of purchase references used to make sure each coin purchase is credited only once. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

2. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's save file in your device backup. That backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account, under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

3. In-app purchases

Coin packs, Quillblade Premium, expansion books and premium cosmetics are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and we never see or receive your name, email address, or payment details. To deliver a purchase, the app receives from Google Play the product you bought, an order number and a purchase token, and uses them on your device only. Google's handling of purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Refunds are requested through Google Play.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Some Legend looks (pens, trails and inks) can be unlocked by watching short video ads, or bought through Google Play instead. After a knockout you can also watch a short video ad to get back up. Watching a reward video is always your choice: it only plays when you tap a button to watch one.

The game also shows a full-screen ad between pages now and then: at most once every three pages cleared, at most once every three minutes, not in your first few games, and never for players who own Quillblade Premium. It can usually be closed after a few seconds.

These ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • information about the device and app, such as model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps, plus crash and performance data for the ad SDK.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Kaselca Apps does not receive this information; we only see total, anonymous ad reports in AdMob.

Consent and your choices. When the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can reopen it at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

The record of which ads you have watched toward each Legend look is stored only on your device, like the rest of your progress.

5. Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations when you take damage or seal enemies. You can turn this off in the game's settings.
  • Google Play Billing: to make in-app purchases.
  • Internet and network state: the game itself runs on your phone and plays offline. Google Play Billing uses your connection to reach Google Play when you buy or restore a purchase, and Google AdMob uses it to load ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so ads can be measured and, where you allow it, personalised.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

6. Data we collect and share

Kaselca Apps does not collect, store on a server, or sell any personal information. The data described in section 4 is collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and shared with Google for advertising, ad measurement, analytics of ad performance and fraud prevention. It is sent encrypted. Your saved game never leaves your device except through Android backup (section 2).

7. Children

Quillblade is a casual game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are requested with Google's settings for family-friendly content (rated G). Purchases are made through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases with Google Family Link or the purchase authentication setting in the Play Store app.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
