Quillblade Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Quillblade ("the app", "we", "us"), made by Kaselca Apps, handles information. In short, we do not collect your personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs other than Google Play Billing.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your phone: unlocked pages, stars, scores, coins, upgrades, cosmetics you own, your settings (sound, music, graphics, vibration), and a short list of purchase references used to make sure each coin purchase is credited only once. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

2. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's save file in your device backup. That backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account, under Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

3. In-app purchases

Coin packs, Quillblade Premium, expansion books and premium cosmetics are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and we never see or receive your name, email address, or payment details. To deliver a purchase, the app receives from Google Play the product you bought, an order number and a purchase token, and uses them on your device only. Google's handling of purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Refunds are requested through Google Play.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations when you take damage or seal enemies. You can turn this off in the game's settings.
  • Google Play Billing: to make in-app purchases.
  • Internet: the app does not use the internet itself. The game runs entirely on your phone; Google Play Billing uses your connection to reach Google Play when you buy or restore a purchase.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

5. Data we collect and share

We do not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. We do not use advertising IDs.

6. Children

Quillblade is a casual game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are made through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases with Google Family Link or the purchase authentication setting in the Play Store app.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
